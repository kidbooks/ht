What this page is

When you engage me for an Art Review, I take on confidentiality obligations. Those obligations are set out in clause B13 of the Terms of Service and in the Art Review Services Agreement. They are deliberately strong, and they are permanent so far as you are concerned: your identity — including your name and your address — your photographs, your documents and the fact that you engaged me at all stay confidential indefinitely.

This page is about the artworks, not about you.

I have already carried out my own independent analysis of the works listed below, and of the further categories described in the next section. That analysis was done prior to, and independently of, any client engagement. It is my own work and my own material. I therefore cannot and do not offer any confidentiality undertaking in respect of these works, and I remain free to discuss, write about, film, publish and otherwise use my findings about them, whenever and however I choose.

If you are considering commissioning an Art Review, read this list first. If the artwork you have in mind appears here, or falls within one of the categories below, I will tell you in writing and you choose what happens next. By default the engagement is declined and your Quotation Fee is refunded in full — you need do nothing for that to happen. Alternatively you may tell me in writing, separately and after I have told you, that you want me to go ahead anyway; in that case the engagement continues, no refund is due on this ground, and no confidentiality undertaking attaches to the artwork itself or to anything I find in it. Your identity, your address and your photographs stay confidential permanently in either case. What changes is the artwork. If you take the default option and the engagement is declined, there is no report and nothing to keep confidential. If instead you tell me to go ahead, then for that engagement no confidentiality attaches to the artwork itself or to anything I find in it, and the five-year confidentiality that clause B13(d) would otherwise give the contents of your report does not apply. See clause B9 of the Terms.

The excluded categories in full

The exclusion covers all of the following:

  • (a) every artwork listed on this page in the table below;
  • (b) the picture book Nicky's Secret: A Christmas Story and every image, illustration and element within it, along with the many artworks that its illustrations allude to, nearly all of which are individually listed on this page anyway;
  • (c) every artwork discussed, shown or referred to in any video, article, or post published on The Hunch Trail YouTube channel, The Hunch Trail Instagram channel, The Hunch Trail Tiktok profile, The Hunch Trail Twitch channel, The Hunch Trail Pinterest page, The Hunch Trail Reddit page, The Hunch Trail X (Twitter) profile, The Hunch Trail LinkedIn page, or The Hunch Trail Facebook page.
  • (d) every artwork discussed, shown or referred to in any article, post, newsletter, video, image, or other material published on any other public channel operated by The Hunch Trail; and
  • (e) any artwork added to this page from time to time.

Whether your artwork is on this list is judged as at the day your engagement is formed. If it is not on the list and does not fall within a category then, it does not become excluded, as against your engagement, because of something I publish or add here afterwards — and the confidentiality you already have is not cut down by any later listing. See clause B9 of the Terms.

I add works to this page from time to time. A work does not need to appear in the table to fall within categories (b), (c), (d) or (e) — the categories operate independently of the list, and the list is not exhaustive of the works I have analysed.

What this page does not do

Listing a work here does not mean:

  • that I claim to have made any discovery about it;
  • that I make any claim about its attribution, authenticity, provenance, condition or value;
  • that I have any interest in it, or any intention to transact in it; or
  • that any opinion I hold about it is correct, complete or capable of verification.

Everything I publish about any artwork is a subjective opinion arrived at by unaided visual examination using a method of my own that has not been tested, peer-reviewed or published in the scientific literature. It is not a valuation, not an authentication, not an attribution, and not advice. See clause A9 of the Terms of Service.

Nothing on this page limits any obligation I have under a law that cannot be excluded by agreement, including any obligation of confidence imposed by a court or by statute.
